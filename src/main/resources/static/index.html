When a downstream microservice is failing or exceeds the 1500ms SLA, OpenMesh-Gateway automatically executes fallback handlers returning standard RFC 7807 problem details:

Inspect /fallback/service-unavailable Inspect /fallback/timeout
Circuit Breaker Policy Summary
  • Sliding Window: 10 requests count-based
  • Failure Threshold: 50% errors trips circuit OPEN
  • Slow Call Threshold: Calls > 1500ms considered failures
  • TimeLimiter: 1500ms maximum duration before cancellation
  • Wait Duration in Open: 10s before transitioning to HALF_OPEN
